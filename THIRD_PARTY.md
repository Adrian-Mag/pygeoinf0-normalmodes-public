# Third-party data

`src/pygeoinf0_normalmodes/earthmodels/data/coastlines.npz`: coastlines derived from GSHHG (Global Self-consistent,
Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database), crude resolution. Wessel, P. and Smith, W. H. F. (1996), A global,
self-consistent, hierarchical, high-resolution shoreline database, J. Geophys. Res., 101(B4), 8741-8743.
